Support

For problems, questions or reports, write to sebastianodemichelis@gmail.com.

Two quick checks before you write

  • On the Mac: System Settings › General › Sharing › Screen Sharing turned on.
  • The address: your device must already reach the Mac — same Wi-Fi network, or a VPN active on both. MacPortal creates no tunnel and works around no router or firewall.
  • The credentials: they are the username and password of your account on the Mac, not a MacPortal account (there is no such thing).

Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

MacPortal collects no personal data, transmits none and stores none. There are no servers, accounts or services run by the developer: the app only opens a direct connection from your device to your Mac.

Data handled

Everything stays on the device:

DataWhere it livesWhy
Name, addresses and port of the Macs you saveThe app's local database, in an App Group shared with the widget and Control CenterTo list and reach your Macs
The Mac's usernameSame local databaseTo pre-fill authentication
The Mac's passwordKeychain only, on the device, reachable only while the device is unlockedTo authenticate the session
The Wi-Fi network name tied to an addressLocal databaseTo try the address that is valid on the current network first
Preferences (quality, clipboard, app lock)The app's local settingsTo remember your choices

The password is never written to the database, to settings or to logs, and is never read by the widget and Control Center extensions.

Data transmission

The only traffic the app generates is the connection to your Mac, to the address and port you enter yourself (5900 by default). That traffic goes from your device to your Mac directly: it passes through no relay and no intermediary. Optionally, if you ask for it, the app sends a Wake-on-LAN packet on the local network.

There is no other network request: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDK transmitting data.

Purchases

MacPortal Pro is handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase system. The developer never sees your payment details: the app only asks the system whether the purchase has been made.

Third parties

The app uses the open source library RoyalVNCKit for the VNC protocol. It runs locally and sends data to nobody.

Permissions requested

  • Local network: to reach the Mac on the same network.
  • Wi-Fi information (optional, iOS): to read the current network name and pick the right address. If you deny it, the app tries the addresses in the order you saved them.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional): for the app lock.

Deleting your data

Deleting a connection also deletes its password from the Keychain. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
